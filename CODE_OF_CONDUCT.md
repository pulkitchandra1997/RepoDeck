# Community Conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique code and ideas rather than people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors. Apply these expectations consistently, give people a chance to correct good-faith mistakes, and keep reports confidential where possible.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an available private contact channel. Before public launch, maintainers must publish a dedicated reporting contact. Do not include sensitive details in public issues while that contact is being arranged.
